Notes on the rooms in the office building

These notes describe the rooms in the six-story office building on the north side of the campus. The facilities team collected them during the spring walk-through, and they are meant to help visitors and staff find a suitable room for meetings, calls and workshops. Room numbers do not always follow the floors, so check the floor listed for each room before you go upstairs. All bookable rooms can be reserved at the reception desk on floor 1 or through the shared booking calendar.

General information

The building opens at seven in the morning and closes at nine in the evening on weekdays. On Saturdays it is open from nine until three, and it is closed on Sundays and public holidays. Visitors must sign in at the reception desk and wear a visitor badge at all times. There are two passenger elevators in the main lobby and a freight elevator at the back of the building near the loading dock. The stairwells are at both ends of each floor. Every floor has accessible restrooms next to the elevators, and drinking water stations are placed near the stairwells.

Floor 1

Floor 1 contains the main lobby, the reception desk, a small café and several rooms for short meetings with visitors.

Room 17 is on floor 1, directly behind the reception desk. It has eight seats around a single oval table, a speakerphone in the middle of the table and a small whiteboard on the side wall. It is the room most often used for interviews and quick visitor meetings, so it is usually booked in thirty-minute slots.

Room 11 is on floor 1, next to the café. It has four soft chairs and a low coffee table. There is no screen, but there are power outlets along the wall. It is meant for informal conversations rather than presentations.

Room 12 is on floor 1, across the hall from Room 11. It has six seats and a wall display with a video conferencing camera. The display can be connected with an HDMI cable, which is kept in the drawer under the table.

Room 15 is on floor 1, at the end of the corridor near the loading dock. It is a storage room for event furniture and is not available for booking.

The café on floor 1 serves coffee, tea and light snacks from eight in the morning until four in the afternoon. Seating in the café is open to everyone and cannot be reserved.

Floor 2

Floor 2 is mainly used for training sessions and team meetings. The kitchen on this floor is shared by everyone in the building.

Room 21 is on floor 2, on the left as you leave the elevators. It has twenty seats arranged in rows, a ceiling projector and a pull-down screen. A wireless microphone and a lectern are stored in the cabinet at the front of the room. This is the largest training room in the building.

Room 22 is on floor 2, next to Room 21. It has ten seats around two joined tables and a portable projector on a cart. The projector must be returned to the cart after each meeting.

Room 24 is on floor 2, beside the kitchen. It has twelve seats and a large wall display, but it has no projector. The room has a sliding glass door and can be noisy at lunch time because of the kitchen next door.

Room 26 is on floor 2, at the far end of the corridor. It has sixteen seats in a U shape, a ceiling projector and two whiteboards. The windows face the parking lot and have blackout blinds for presentations.

Room 28 is on floor 2, opposite Room 26. It is a quiet room with two seats and a desk for phone calls. It has no screen and no projector.

The kitchen on floor 2 has two refrigerators, a dishwasher, a microwave oven and a coffee machine. Please label any food you leave in the refrigerators. The refrigerators are emptied every Friday afternoon.

Floor 3

Floor 3 is used by the product and design teams. Most of the floor is open seating, with meeting rooms along the east side.

Room 42 is on floor 3. It has twelve seats, a whiteboard that covers the whole back wall and a wide window that faces the courtyard. It is the brightest room on the floor and is popular for design reviews. There is a wall display with a video conferencing camera next to the door.

Room 31 is on floor 3, near the elevators. It has six seats and a small wall display. The table has built-in power outlets and cable covers.

Room 33 is on floor 3, between Room 31 and the stairwell. It has four seats, a whiteboard and a speakerphone. It is often used for short stand-up meetings.

Room 35 is on floor 3, in the north corner. It is a workshop room with movable tables, thirty stackable chairs, three rolling whiteboards and a ceiling projector. Before a workshop, ask the facilities team to arrange the tables.

Room 37 is on floor 3, next to Room 35. It is a small library with shelves of reference books and two reading chairs. It cannot be booked, but anyone may use it quietly.

Floor 4

Floor 4 is used by the finance and administration teams. Access to this floor requires a staff badge, so visitors must be accompanied.

Room 41 is on floor 4, beside the elevators. It has ten seats, a wall display and a speakerphone. It is mostly used for monthly planning meetings.

Room 43 is on floor 4, at the end of the west corridor. It has eight seats and a whiteboard. The room has no windows, which keeps it cool in the summer.

Room 45 is on floor 4, across from Room 43. It is a records room with locked cabinets and is not available for booking.

Room 47 is on floor 4, next to the stairwell. It is a quiet room with one seat and a desk for private calls.

Floor 5

Floor 5 is used by the engineering teams. It has open seating, a small lounge and several meeting rooms of different sizes.

Room 51 is on floor 5, near the lounge. It has fourteen seats, a ceiling projector and a pull-down screen. There is also a wall display with a video conferencing camera for remote participants.

Room 53 is on floor 5, next to Room 51. It has six seats, two whiteboards and a standing desk.

Room 55 is on floor 5, in the south corner. It has eight seats and a wall display. The room is close to the server room, so it can be slightly warm.

Room 57 is on floor 5, opposite Room 55. It is a quiet room with two seats and a desk for calls.

The lounge on floor 5 has sofas, a table football game and a small refrigerator with cold drinks.

Floor 6

Floor 6 is the top floor. It has a large event hall, a roof terrace and two meeting rooms for guests.

Room 61 is on floor 6, next to the event hall. It has twelve seats around a long wooden table, a wall display and a speakerphone.

Room 63 is on floor 6, beside the roof terrace. It has six seats and a view over the city. It has no screen.

The event hall on floor 6 has up to one hundred and twenty seats, a stage, a ceiling projector with two large screens and a sound system. The hall must be booked at least two weeks in advance through the facilities team.

The roof terrace is open in good weather from April to October. It has tables, chairs and sunshades, and it closes at sunset.

Safety

Fire exits are marked with green signs at both ends of every floor. In an emergency, do not use the elevators; walk down the nearest stairwell and gather in the courtyard in front of the main entrance. First aid kits are kept at the reception desk and in the kitchen on floor 2, and a defibrillator is mounted on the wall next to the elevators on floor 3. Fire drills take place twice a year, and the date is announced in the booking calendar one week in advance. If you notice a hazard, such as a blocked exit or a broken light in a stairwell, please tell the reception desk as soon as possible.

Parking and bicycles

The parking lot behind the building has forty spaces for staff and six marked spaces for visitors near the loading dock. Bicycle racks are under the canopy by the main entrance, and lockers for helmets are available on floor 1 next to the café.

Booking rules

Meetings in any bookable room should not be longer than three hours unless the facilities team agrees. If a meeting is canceled, please release the room in the booking calendar so that others can use it. Rooms that are not used within fifteen minutes of the start of a booking are released automatically. After each meeting, clean the whiteboards, switch off the displays and projectors, and leave the chairs around the tables.

Equipment and help

Spare HDMI and USB-C adapters can be borrowed from the reception desk on floor 1. If a projector, display or speakerphone does not work, report it to the facilities team by phone or at the reception desk. Lost and found items are kept at the reception desk for thirty days.

Cleaning and maintenance

The cleaning team visits every floor in the evening after the building closes. Meeting rooms on floors 2 and 3 are also cleaned at lunch time because they are used the most. Maintenance work is usually scheduled on Saturday mornings, and any rooms that will be closed are listed in the booking calendar a week ahead.
