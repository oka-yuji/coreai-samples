Modification: Encoding only, from Windows-1252 (CP1252) to UTF-8.
CRLF line endings, headers, rows, units and values are unchanged.
No imputation or aggregation was performed.

These are observed rentals and historical observed weather in Asia/Seoul.
The 295 Functioning Day=No rows record zero rentals during shutdown;
they do not establish zero latent demand. Weather is not a future forecast.
No forecast-quality claim is made.
