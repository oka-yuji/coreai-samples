Scoring guide

0 — Failed: the requirement is not met. 1 — Acceptable: the requirement is met with visible defects. 2 — Good: the requirement is clearly met. Leave uncertain or unchecked axes unreviewed.

Instruction: requested content and changes. Detail: shape, edges, anatomy and fine features. Text: exact requested lettering and legibility. Preservation: reference content outside the requested change. Alpha: requested transparency and clean edges. Use the background selector when assessing transparency.

The acceptance calculation runs separately. This page never grants model verification or replaces the numerical checks.
